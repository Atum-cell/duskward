How to play

  • Orbit the sunHold ← → or A / D to circle around the sun.
  • Slip through the gapsWalls close in from the edges. Find the opening in each one. Your score is how long you survive.
  • Outlast the dayThe sky runs from dawn to midnight. Each new time of day spins a little faster.
  • Charge your shieldBrush past the edge of a wall to fill the ring around your orbit. A full ring saves you from one hit.
  • Watch for solar flaresFrom about 20 seconds, walls burst out of the sun. A dashed outline shows where they'll be, so move to its gap.
  • The sun changes shapeIt can become a pentagon or a square, and the walls change with it. Fewer sides means wider gaps but longer moves.
  • Pick a difficultyEasy, Normal and Hard each have their own best times and leaderboard.

Tips pop up the first time each of these happens.
